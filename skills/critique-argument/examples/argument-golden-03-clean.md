# Recommendation: Move the Weekly Status Meeting to Async Written Updates

We recommend replacing the weekly status meeting with an async written update, starting next
sprint.

Attendance logs for the last quarter show that of twelve scheduled meetings, average attendance
was six of fourteen team members. The shared meeting notes document shows that only three people
ever added a comment after the fact, and no one edited the notes after the week they were posted.

A status ritual that most of the team skips, and that almost no one revisits afterward, is not
actually creating shared awareness across the team. Since the meeting's stated purpose is shared
awareness rather than live discussion, replacing it with a format more people actually read and
respond to should raise how many team members stay current, even though this quarter's data alone
cannot prove that a written update will outperform a meeting nobody attends.

Some team members will likely argue that dropping the meeting removes a channel for catching
problems in real time. To keep that channel without keeping the meeting's low attendance, we
propose a standing fifteen-minute optional call each week for anyone who wants to raise something
live, alongside the written update everyone is expected to read.
