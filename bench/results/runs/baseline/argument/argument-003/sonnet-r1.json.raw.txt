Location: "Why the Pilot Result Applies Systemwide," first paragraph
Evidence: "Removing the financial penalty for late returns predictably increases both on-time return rates and overall program participation, which is the reason the pilot's return-rate and avoidance-survey results, taken together, support ending fines for the whole consortium."
Problem: The claim that the effect is "predictable" is used as the reason to trust the pilot result and generalize it, while the pilot result is offered as the evidence for the claim being predictable; this is circular and does not independently establish that the remaining five branches will behave like the pilot branches.
Fix: Support the "predictably increases" claim with evidence independent of this pilot, such as the cited research brief's findings, before using it to justify extending the result to all ten branches.
