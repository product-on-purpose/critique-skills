Location: Archive this project? section, the "Archive" control (id="archive-confirm-archive-link")
Evidence: <a id="archive-confirm-archive-link" href="#project-archived">Archive</a>
Problem: A destructive, state-changing action is implemented as a hyperlink rather than a button. Assistive technology announces it as navigation rather than an action, and it can be triggered accidentally through middle-click, right-click-open-in-new-tab, or link prefetching.
Fix: Change "Archive" to a <button type="submit"> wired to the archive action instead of an anchor tag.
