Location: Line 44, custom control element
Evidence: <div class="icon-delete" role="button" tabindex="0" aria-label="Delete item" id="s3-customcontrol"><img src="/img/trash-icon.svg" alt=""></div>
Problem: A div with role="button" lacks keyboard event handlers. While tabindex makes it focusable, a div cannot natively respond to Enter and Space keys like a native button does, making it inaccessible to keyboard users.
Fix: Replace the div with a native <button> element, or add onkeypress handlers to respond to Enter and Space keys.
