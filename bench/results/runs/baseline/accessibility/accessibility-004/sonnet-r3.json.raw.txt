Location: "Delete item" control in the Fare adjustments section, id="s3-customcontrol"
Evidence: "<div class="icon-delete" role="button" tabindex="0" aria-label="Delete item" id="s3-customcontrol">" wrapping an image with an empty alt attribute, with no visible item or label nearby indicating what it deletes.
Problem: A native button element provides keyboard activation, focus handling, and role semantics automatically; reimplementing this with a div and role="button" is easy to get wrong, and there is no surrounding content indicating what item this control would delete.
Fix: Replace the div with a native "button type="button"" element, and add visible context for what item it acts on, or remove the control if nothing on the page corresponds to it.

Location: step indicator, id="s3-wizard" and its three child divs (id="s3-step1", id="s3-step2", id="s3-step3")
