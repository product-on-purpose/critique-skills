Location: `<meta name="viewport" ...>` in `<head>`
Evidence: `content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, user-scalable=no"`
Problem: `user-scalable=no` disables pinch-to-zoom, blocking low-vision users from magnifying content and violating WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
Fix: Remove `user-scalable=no` (and any `maximum-scale=1`) from the viewport meta tag so users can zoom.

Location: `<title id="head-title">` vs `<h1 id="main-h1">`
Evidence: Title is "Cascadia Regional Transit: Service Announcements" while the H1 reads "Fare and service announcements"
Problem: The page title and main heading describe the page differently (one omits "Fare", wording otherwise diverges), which is confusing for users and bookmarks/history entries.
Fix: Align the `<title>` wording with the `<h1>`, e.g. "Cascadia Regional Transit: Fare and Service Announcements".

Location: Section `s1`, paragraph `s1-p1`
Evidence: `style="color:#999999"` on white background
Problem: #999999 on white gives a contrast ratio of roughly 2.85:1, failing WCAG AA's 4.5:1 minimum for normal text.
Fix: Remove the inline color override or change it to a color that meets at least 4.5:1 contrast (e.g. #595959 or darker) against the page background.

Location: Section `s3`, `s3-statuscard`

Location: Section `s3`, `s3-button1`
