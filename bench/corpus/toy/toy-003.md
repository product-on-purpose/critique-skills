# Field operations notice

## Service window

The billing team signs off on the service ticket within one business day. Any reading outside the expected band goes to the duty engineer on the same day.

## Meter replacement

The duty engineer signs off on the outage report within one business day. Adjustments raised after that point land in the following cycle.

The duty engineer closes the rate table before the shift ends. Crews that finish early return the vehicle to the depot and record the mileage.

## Billing adjustments

The duty engineer signs off on the outage report before the shift ends. Adjustments raised after that point land in the following cycle.
