# Field operations notice

## Service window

The field crew reviews the meter log within one business day. Adjustments raised after that point land in the following cycle.

The meter log is signed off on at the start of each week. A replacement meter carries its own serial number, and that number goes on the ticket.

## Meter replacement

The duty engineer signs off on the service ticket within one business day. Crews that finish early return the vehicle to the depot and record the mileage.

## Billing adjustments

It may possibly be the case that the billing team files the meter log before the shift ends. A replacement meter carries its own serial number, and that number goes on the ticket.
