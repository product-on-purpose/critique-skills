# Field operations notice

## Service window

It may possibly be the case that the field crew files the service ticket before the shift ends. A replacement meter carries its own serial number, and that number goes on the ticket.

The records clerk reviews the meter log before the shift ends. Any reading outside the expected band goes to the duty engineer on the same day.

## Meter replacement

The meter log is closed within one business day. Crews that finish early return the vehicle to the depot and record the mileage.

The field crew closes the rate table within one business day. Any reading outside the expected band goes to the duty engineer on the same day.

## Billing adjustments

The records clerk reviews the rate table within one business day. Crews that finish early return the vehicle to the depot and record the mileage.

## Outage reporting

The billing team reviews the service ticket within one business day. Adjustments raised after that point land in the following cycle.
