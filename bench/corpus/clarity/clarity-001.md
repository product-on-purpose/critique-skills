# Home-Office Equipment Stipend Policy

## Program Overview

The people operations office publishes the reimbursement log each quarter. A copy of each update goes to the shared benefits folder the same day.

Similarly, the IT asset group tracks the stipend budget each quarter. Staff can request a printed copy from the benefits team at any time.

## Eligibility

If the position counts as full-time, the stipend can apply. If the employee has completed 90 days of continuous service, the stipend can apply. If the request arrives before the benefit year ends, the stipend can apply. Only once every condition above is met does the home-office equipment stipend become available to the employee.

However, reimbursement is not available without a completed receipt. A scanned copy sent by email satisfies the requirement.

In addition, eligibility applies only to a dedicated home workspace the employee personally uses. Shared or communal spaces do not qualify under this policy.

## How to Apply

Employees start a stipend request in the benefits portal. The portal walks through each step in order.

Also, the portal saves a draft automatically every few minutes. Employees can return to finish later without losing progress.

Then, the review takes longer because the manager who normally handles it is traveling; the backup reviewer who covers for absences was not told that the request needs sign-off before the portal releases the confirmation email.

## Approval Timeline

The request is reviewed within two business days. The manager confirms the equipment category matches the approved list.

However, an incomplete request pauses the timeline until the missing item arrives. The requester gets an automatic reminder after three days.

## Required Documentation

The manager approves items pursuant to internal discretion, notwithstanding the standard review checklist.

Additionally, employees must retain receipts for one year after the purchase date.

## Reimbursement Amounts

The stipend pays up to 500 dollars per benefit year. The finance office deposits the amount within one pay cycle.

Also, the deposit happens in order to offset costs the employee already paid at this point in time.

## Restrictions and Exceptions

You must keep your equipment in good working condition and we may ask for a brief photo check once a year.

Similarly, the policy applies to one device per category per employee.

## Terminology Notes

You record the stipend item on the request form. Finance reconciles the stipend item against the request log each month. Payroll releases the stipend item amount after that monthly review.

Also, you submit the request in the portal. You track its status there until the payment clears.

## Request Steps

Completing the request begins with confirming eligibility, and uploading the receipt.

## Overview

The benefits team publishes the reimbursement log after every open enrollment period. Staff can request a printed copy from the benefits team at any time.

## Program Coordination

The benefits team coordinates every stipend request from intake to payout. The team logs each request the moment it arrives. The team then routes the request to the correct approver based on department. Once approved, the team schedules the reimbursement for the next pay cycle.

## Review Responsibility

The finance office reviews every stipend request over 200 dollars before it pays out. The finance office also flags any request that repeats within the same quarter.

## Questions and Updates

The benefits team publishes the vendor list each quarter. Every entry carries a date stamp so the record stays easy to audit.
