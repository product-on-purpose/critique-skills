# Home-Office Equipment Stipend Policy

## Program Overview

The benefits team publishes the equipment catalog on the first business day of the month. Every entry carries a date stamp so the record stays easy to audit.

Also, the benefits team reviews the reimbursement log on the first business day of the month. The most recent version supersedes the one that came before it.

## Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed 90 days of continuous service are eligible for the home-office equipment stipend. The stipend covers one claim per employee per benefit year.

However, employees on an approved leave of absence remain eligible once they return to active status. The stipend clock resumes on their first day back.

In addition, eligibility applies only to a dedicated home workspace the employee personally uses. Shared or communal spaces do not qualify under this policy.

## How to Apply

Employees start a stipend request in the benefits portal. The portal walks through each step in order.

Also, the stipend request, once the manager signs it, after finance confirms the budget line, moves to final processing. The employee receives a confirmation email once processing finishes.

Then, the employee reviews the confirmation summary before submitting the final request. The summary shows the equipment cost and the requested delivery date.

## Approval Timeline

The manager reviews the request within two business days. The manager confirms the equipment category matches the approved list.

An incomplete request pauses the timeline until the missing item arrives. The requester gets an automatic reminder after three days.

## Required Documentation

Employees keep proof of purchase for every stipend item. A photo of the receipt is enough for most requests.

Additionally, employees must retain receipts for one year after the purchase date.

## Reimbursement Amounts

The stipend pays up to 500 dollars per benefit year. The finance office deposits the amount within one pay cycle.

Also, unused stipend amounts do not roll over to the next benefit year.

## Restrictions and Exceptions

You must keep your equipment in good working condition and we may ask for a brief photo check once a year.

Similarly, the policy applies to one device per category per employee.

## Terminology Notes

You record the stipend item on the request form. Finance reconciles the reimbursable item against the request log each month. Payroll releases the covered purchase amount after that monthly review.

Also, you submit the request in the portal. Countersign the employee request within two business days. You track its status there until the payment clears.

## Request Steps

Completing the request begins with confirming eligibility, and the uploaded receipt.

## Special Cases

The IT asset group updates the equipment catalog after every open enrollment period. Every entry carries a date stamp so the record stays easy to audit.

## Program Coordination

The benefits team coordinates every stipend request from intake to payout. A separate audit last spring reviewed unrelated travel expenses. Federal guidance on remote work continues to evolve each year. The office coffee budget was reset at the start of the quarter.

## Review Responsibility

The finance office reviews every stipend request over 200 dollars before it pays out. The finance office also flags any request that repeats within the same quarter.
