# v0.2.0 shape options: a decision brief

**Status:** RULED 2026-09-11. v0.2.0 is **Option A plus Option C**.
**Blocked since:** 2026-08-08. **Unblocked:** 2026-09-11.
**Recorded:** 2026-09-11, from the backlog-expansion intake.
**Tracked as:** [E1 (rule the v0.2.0 shape)](../backlog/enhancements.md#e1---rule-the-v020-shape-pick-one-of-the-four-options).

This document was written to make one decision decidable. It has since been ruled; the ruling is
recorded immediately below, and the four options are kept beneath it unedited, because the
reasoning that was rejected is part of the record of why the chosen shape was chosen.

## The ruling, 2026-09-11

**v0.2.0 is Option A plus Option C: receipts first, and the research spine.** It contains all of
Option A's measurement debt, plus E44 (the taxonomy survey) and N1 (critique-deck).

**What moved:**

| Item | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| E44 (taxonomy survey) | v0.3.0 | **v0.2.0** |
| N1 (critique-deck) | v0.3.0 | **v0.2.0** |
| N3 (critique-dataviz) | v0.4.0+ | **unscheduled**, held in the backlog |
| N2 (critique-forms) | v0.3.0 | unchanged |
| E45 (BYOR), E48 (telemetry) | v0.3.0 | unchanged |

**N3 (critique-dataviz) is deliberately not dated.** It is intended for after v0.2.0, but the
chart-spec generator it depends on has not been scoped, and dating a skill before its blocker is
sized is a guess. It stays accepted in `new-components.md` rather than being dropped. Taking it
out of the exit gate is the specific thing that makes v0.2.0 closable: it was the one XL item
holding a gate that transitively required all eight roadmap items.

**The exit gate for v0.2.0 is therefore Option A's gate, plus Option C's two additions:**
the survey published at `docs/internal/research/critique-framework-survey.md` with a recorded
gate verdict per candidate, and `critique-deck` measured at k=5 on both pinned tiers and present
in the README scoreboard. Every clause of Option A's half is machine-checkable; these two are
artifact-existence checks. Nothing in the gate depends on N3.

**Still open, and now on the critical path.** Option C's skill half cannot be measured on both
tiers until the sonnet tier works, and E2's root cause turned out to be unidentified rather than
one line (see the correction recorded on E2). `--timeout` shipped on 2026-09-11; the underlying
failure needs a fresh trace. **E2 now gates the v0.2.0 exit gate**, where under Option A alone it
only gated three measurement items.

The three riders in "Whichever option is chosen" at the foot of this document still apply and
are not discharged by this ruling.

## Why this is the root node

`ROADMAP.md:9` makes every version sequence-gated: v0.2.0 does not open until v0.1.x's exit gate
closes, and v0.3.0 does not open until v0.2.0's does. `ROADMAP.md:70` then makes v0.2.0's exit gate
transitively require all eight of its items, including `critique-dataviz`, which is blocked on a
chart-spec corpus generator mode that does not exist. The practical effect is that **a single XL
sub-item holds the gate on a version that has been nominally open for over a month and has produced
no v0.2.0 work.**

The deferral is visible in the record rather than inferred from it:

- `docs/internal/release-plans/` holds only `plan_v0.1.0/`. No v0.2.0 plan has been started.
- `RELEASE-NOTES.md:5-7` says "Nothing yet" while `CHANGELOG.md`'s `[Unreleased]` carries roughly
  35 bullets. That is the largest body of unreleased work the project has held.
- The sonnet fix, an open decision since v0.1.6, has stayed unmade partly because its payoff
  (usability re-measure, consistency threshold v2, the sonnet fidelity gate) is v0.2.0 content whose
  shape is unknown.
- Two commitments that appeared in the pre-build local plan, `askit-*` authoring and CodeQL, vanished
  in the rewrite to the public `ROADMAP.md` with no recorded decision to cut them
  ([E11](../backlog/enhancements.md)).

Nine of the ten largest items in the backlog cannot be ordered until this is ruled.

## Two things have overtaken the 2026-08-08 proposal

The original recommendation was a three-way split: v0.2 = BYOR plus measurement debt plus threshold
v2; v0.3 = survey then the skill wave; v0.4 = samples then the Astro site. Do not adopt it as
written, for two reasons found since:

1. **The site shipped on 2026-08-22.** The whole of that proposal's v0.4 content is one item lighter.
2. **BYOR's "cheapest item" premise is true of the contract layer only.** The audit confirmed the
   schema, the `BYOR-*` criterion namespace, `skills/_shared/merge.py:130-145` and the frontmatter
   validator already handle a BYOR finding with zero code change. It also found that
   `agents/critique-critic.md:28-33` is closed to four parameters with no rubric channel,
   `scripts/checks.py` is hard-wired to fixed criterion sets, no `SKILL.md` mentions `byor`, and no
   flagship skill has been named. The cheap half is real; the invocation surface does not exist.

There is also an unresolved question underneath BYOR that no option can dodge: `ROADMAP.md:113`
rules out any skill that cannot be measured, and nobody has ruled how a rubric supplied at runtime,
which cannot be seeded into a corpus, is measured or why it is exempt
([E10](../backlog/enhancements.md)).

## The four options

Each is mutually exclusive with the others, names the backlog items it contains, and states an exit
gate you could actually check.

### Option A - Receipts first (measurement debt only, no new component)

**Thesis.** Make every receipt the project already publishes whole before adding any new claim.
v0.2.0 changes the numbers, not the catalog.

**Contains.** E2 (sonnet unblock), E5 (31-file fabricated metadata), E6 (run_set and lane schema
v1.2.0), E7 (steering directory move), E19 (fidelity gate on sonnet), E20 (usability sonnet
re-measure), E21 (haiku-r1 backfill), E22 (severity_expected scoring), E23 (methodology
location-level section), E24 (evidence-quotes check), E25 (verdicts.md rewrite), E26 (consistency
threshold v2), E27 (harness isolation), E28 (CLI version pin), plus the ready-now doc fixes E3, E4,
E8 and E9. E11 (askit and CodeQL ruling) and E12 (exit-gate re-cut) are the release's paperwork.
Survey, BYOR, the skill wave and telemetry move to v0.3.0; samples to v0.4.0+.

**Exit gate.** Every entry in `bench/results/results.json` carries `run_set` and `lane`, and
`bench.report` drift-checks the judged and scripted columns. At least one sonnet envelope per skill
is committed through the shipped `run_bench.py` harness and the ADR 0031 (fidelity gate acceptance
band) verdict is re-evaluated on that tier. Consistency threshold v2 is published with its method.
Zero `run.model` values outside the two pinned tiers, or a documented sentinel, under `skills/` and
`examples/`, enforced by a check. `bench/results/verdicts.md` is one current-state document.
`severity_expected` is scored in the table. The evidence-quotes check runs in CI.
`bench/results/runs/steering/` no longer sits inside the `runs*` glob. A recorded ruling exists on
`askit-*` authoring and CodeQL.

**Ships when.** Roughly 10 to 14 sessions, plus wall clock for sonnet dispatches to complete before
threshold v2 can be calibrated. The critical path is E2, then E19, then E26.

**For.** It is the only shape whose every item can start today with zero further decisions, and
whose exit gate is machine-checkable end to end. It makes the sonnet tier, half the published
measurement basis, reproducible through the shipped harness for the first time. Three of its items
are the RC-HANDOVER defects confirmed still open, and six are the measurement debt `ROADMAP.md:58`
already promised to close "here rather than carried further".

**Against.** No user-visible feature and no positioning change. It abandons `ROADMAP.md:46`'s
"widening the skill base" framing for v0.2.0 outright, and a version number spent entirely on debt
can read as stalling. The survey, called the single unlocking item since 2026-08-17, slips another
full version.

### Option B - Receipts plus BYOR (the 2026-08-08 split, updated)

**Thesis.** Ship one positioning change, that a user can bring their own rubric, on top of whole
receipts, because the contract half is already built.

**Contains.** Everything in Option A, plus E10 (rule how BYOR is measured) and E45 (BYOR on one
named flagship skill, with a golden example and a recipe). A flagship must be named; none is chosen
anywhere in the repository today. Survey, skill wave, samples and telemetry move to v0.3.0+.

**Exit gate.** Option A's gate, plus: BYOR documented and shipped on one named skill with at least
one golden example under `skills/<flagship>/examples/` carrying `rubric_source: byor`, one
`examples/recipes/` entry, an `agents/critique-critic.md` invocation contract that accepts a rubric,
and a recorded ADR on how a runtime-supplied rubric is measured or why it is exempt from the
measured-only rule.

**Ships when.** Roughly 14 to 20 sessions: Option A plus three to five for the BYOR invocation
layer, and the E10 ruling has to land before BYOR can open.

**For.** `ROADMAP.md:56` calls BYOR the pattern-setter for every later skill, and the contract, the
criterion namespace, the merge path and the validator already accept a BYOR finding with no code
change.

**Against.** The cheap-item premise covers the contract layer only. The critic subagent is closed to
four parameters, the scripted checks are hard-wired, no skill mentions `byor`, and no flagship is
named, so v0.2.0 would depend on a decision not yet made and an invocation surface with no existing
code. It also ships a component the exit gate's "measured to the same bar" clause may not be able to
admit, which is exactly what E10 is about.

### Option C - Survey-led (the research spine first, as `ROADMAP.md` currently orders it)

**Thesis.** Regenerate the research spine before widening anything, and prove the skill template on
one new skill.

**Contains.** E44 (taxonomy survey, including a Two-Part Gate verdict on the external audit's four
architectural recommendations and five candidate skills), N1 (critique-deck, which hard-requires E30
and E2 and a corpus module), and Option A's measurement subset: E2, E5, E6, E7, E19, E20, E22, E23,
E24, E25, E27, E28. BYOR, forms, dataviz, samples, telemetry and threshold v2 move to v0.3.0+.

**Exit gate.** `docs/internal/research/critique-framework-survey.md` published with a recorded gate
verdict per candidate; `critique-deck` measured at k=5 on both pinned tiers and present in the README
scoreboard with its own corpus domain, criterion table, joint-routing cases and golden examples; the
measurement subset closed as in Option A; the v1.0 readiness tracker populated from the survey.

**Ships when.** Roughly 17 to 22 sessions: three to four for the survey, four to six for
`critique-deck` end to end, eight to ten for the measurement subset. The sonnet unblock must land
before deck can be measured.

**For.** It is the item `ROADMAP.md:48` lists first and says gates the survey-scale claim. The
survey's absence has left the external audit's candidate slate with no institutional verdict. A
seventh skill is the only thing that actually validates the skill-template and joint-routing costs
the audit found before the wider wave pays them three times.

**Against.** The survey is research with no code to anchor it and no candidate list exists to size
it. `critique-deck` is L and pulls in the corpus module, joint-routing rework of six existing
descriptions, and two-tier measurement that is itself blocked on E2. It is the longest of the three
bounded shapes and the closest to the as-written failure mode.

### Option D - As written (keep the eight-item v0.2.0 and its published exit gate)

**Thesis.** Honor the public sequence exactly as published and change nothing about the promise.

**Contains.** Every v0.2.0 item in `ROADMAP.md:48-64`, including N3 (critique-dataviz) and its new
generator mode, plus a ruling on E11 and E12, since even the as-written gate omits the two vanished
commitments.

**Exit gate.** Exactly `ROADMAP.md:70`: survey published; every listed skill measured; BYOR
documented and shipped on one skill; docs site deployed (met 2026-08-22); every new component built
and measured to the v0.1.0 bar, which transitively includes the dataviz chart-spec corpus.

**Ships when.** 45 or more sessions, spanning months. Disposition telemetry additionally needs real
usage volume that no session count can buy.

**For.** No roadmap rewrite and no public change of promise. It is the only shape that does not
require explaining a re-cut exit gate to readers who took `ROADMAP.md` at its word.

**Against.** The gate can run for months on the strength of one XL sub-item blocked on
infrastructure that does not exist. This is the shape that has actually been in force for 35 days
and produced no v0.2.0 work. And it is not really the status quo either, because the vanished
`askit-*` and CodeQL commitments need a ruling either way.

## Recommendation as written before the ruling

*Kept for the record. The maintainer ruled A plus C, taking this recommendation and adding
Option C's survey and first skill on top of it.*

**Option A, receipts first.** The single strongest reason: it is the only shape whose exit gate the
maintainer can begin closing today with zero further decisions, because every item in it is
unblocked and every gate clause is machine-checkable, a field in `results.json`, a sonnet envelope on
disk, zero non-pinned model IDs under `skills/` and `examples/`. For a project whose entire argument
is receipts, making the sonnet tier reproducible through the shipped harness for the first time is
worth more than any positioning change.

Make the E10 ruling (how BYOR is measured) during v0.2.0 at zero build cost, so Option B's content
can open v0.3.0 immediately rather than starting from an undecided question.

Recommending is not deciding. The ruling is the maintainer's.

## Whichever option is chosen

Three things hold across all four, and should be part of the ruling rather than left to follow it:

1. **Rule E11 explicitly.** `askit-*` authoring and CodeQL were committed in the local plan and
   disappeared in the public rewrite with no recorded cut. Two of the four options above would let
   that happen a second time by simply not mentioning them. Cut them on the record or schedule them;
   do not let silence decide again.
2. **Re-cut the exit gate to match (E12).** Options A, B and C all imply a gate different from
   `ROADMAP.md:70`. The gate is a public promise, so the edit is part of the decision, not cleanup
   after it.
3. **The v0.1.x exit-gate declaration is separate and comes first.** Zero open issues and 25 days
   since the last defect fix mean the gate reads as met on any reading; what it needs is a
   declaration, not a decision. Worth settling at the same time whether "no open issues" is how you
   want to measure it, since an empty tracker also measures reporting volume
   ([E14](../backlog/enhancements.md)).
